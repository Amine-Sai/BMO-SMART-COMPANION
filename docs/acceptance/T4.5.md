# Acceptance Test T4.5: Idle lifecycle & VRAM unload

## Status: PASS

## Scope
- Idle lifecycle manager tracking activity timestamps and brain idle status.
- Automatic module deactivation and LLM VRAM unloading after `idle_unload_s` of inactivity in an empty room (`BrainState.IDLE` and `len(scene.people) == 0`).
- Reactive wake-up on person detection or user activity: reactivates face/sign modules (`face=True, sign=True`) and prewarms LLM.
- Prevents unloading while people are detected in the room or state is non-idle.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. When room is empty and brain state is `IDLE`, ticking after `idle_unload_s` sets `sleeping=True`, turns off face/sign modules, and unloads LLM.
2. `PersonDetected` event or `notify_activity()` restores `sleeping=False`, restores vision modules, and prewarms LLM.
3. Does not unload when people are present in scene or brain state is not `IDLE`.
